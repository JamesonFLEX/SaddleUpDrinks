Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Saddle Up Drinks is a family-owned luxury mobile bar serving the greater Sacramento area. This policy explains what information our website, saddleupdrinks.com, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us through the quote request form:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Event details: occasion, date, guest count, city or venue, the services and add-ons you select
  • Anything you write in the notes field

Our website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or tracking tools, and it does not ask you to create an account.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, prepare your quote and custom menu, and plan your event
  • To contact you about your booking

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else receives it

We use a small number of service providers to run the website:

  • Formspree (formspree.io) receives your form submission and delivers it to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces on this site, so your browser connects to Google's servers to load them.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your request for as long as we need it to respond, plan your event and keep normal business records. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

To review, correct or delete the information you sent us, contact us using the details below and we will take care of it.

Do Not Track

Our website does not track visitors across other websites, so browser "Do Not Track" signals do not change how this site works.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Alcohol service is for guests 21 and older.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
