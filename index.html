Tell us the date, we’ll bring the bar.

Four quick steps and your request lands with the Saddle Up family. We'll come back with a custom menu and quote for your event.

How much does a booking cost?

Packages start at $600, with a $600 minimum booking. Your quote will reflect your selected services, event details, and any add-ons.

How do I reserve my date?

A $200 deposit holds your date. The remaining balance is due one week before your event.

How far in advance should I book?

There is no fixed booking deadline. If your date is available, we welcome last-minute requests too.

Service area

We serve Sacramento and surrounding areas. Travel within 50 miles is included. For events farther away, we provide a travel quote based on distance.

Can I book just the coffee and matcha bar?

Yes. Book the Gourmet Coffee Bar on its own or add it to any package.

What if the trailer does not fit?

We can bring a portable bar setup instead. The trailer needs about 17 feet of space; share your venue details so we can plan the setup.

What about power and rain?

Share your venue’s power details when you inquire so we can confirm the setup. A generator is available for an additional $75. The trailer is enclosed, and we will discuss the event layout and weather plan with you.

Can I arrange a tasting?

Yes, tastings are available. Ask us about options when planning your event.

Can you help with a corporate event?

Yes. Ask about coffee, matcha, and bar service, branded cups, insurance certificates, and formal invoices.
