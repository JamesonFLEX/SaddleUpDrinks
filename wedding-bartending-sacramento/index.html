Good to know

Questions we hear most.

Who provides the alcohol?

With our Full-Service Bar Experience, Saddle Up provides the alcohol under our ABC catering license. With Mobile Bartending Services, you purchase and provide the alcohol, and our team handles the bar service.

How much does it cost?

Packages start at $600, with a $600 minimum booking. Your quote reflects your selected services, event details, and any add-ons. A $200 deposit holds your date, and the balance is due one week before your event.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Saddle Up carries general liability insurance and an ABC catering license, and our bartenders are RBS-certified.

Can we arrange a tasting?

Yes, tastings are available. Ask us about options when planning your event.

What if the trailer does not fit at our venue?

The trailer needs about 17 feet of space. If it will not fit, we bring our portable bar instead. Share your venue details so we can plan the setup.

What about power and rain?

Share your venue’s power details when you inquire so we can confirm the setup. A generator is available for an additional $75. The trailer is enclosed, and we discuss the event layout and weather plan with you.

How far in advance should we book?

There is no fixed booking deadline. If your date is available, we welcome last-minute requests too.

Do you travel outside Sacramento?

We serve Sacramento and surrounding areas, and travel within 50 miles is included. Roseville, Rocklin, Folsom, Elk Grove, Davis and Granite Bay all sit inside that radius. For events farther away, we provide a travel quote based on distance.
